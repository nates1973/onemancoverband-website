A few helpful details

Frequently asked questions

Do you perform Christian music?

Yes. As a former worship leader, I can perform hymns, worship songs, and other faith-based music when requested.

Can you perform at both the ceremony and reception?

Absolutely. Many couples combine multiple services into a customized wedding-day package.

Can you learn a special song for our wedding?

Often, yes. If a particular song is important to your celebration, let me know and we'll discuss whether it can be incorporated.

Do you take requests?

When appropriate, yes. With a repertoire of more than 500 songs, I can often accommodate guest requests throughout the event.

Can live music replace a DJ?

For many weddings, yes. Some couples choose a live acoustic experience instead of a traditional DJ. We can discuss whether that approach fits your reception.

Do you travel for weddings?

Yes. I'm available throughout the Denver metro area, Front Range communities, and Colorado mountain destinations. Travel fees may apply depending on venue location and event requirements.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates can fill quickly, especially during Colorado's peak wedding season. Reach out as soon as possible to check availability and begin planning.
